1i

Colour legend & notation

16-colour ANSI plus the dim attribute (SGR 2). Nothing depends on the background — every mark is a glyph in a foreground colour
Every colour, and what it encodes
black · 0#161826The ground. Mooshik never paints a background — this is just what is behind.
bright black · 8#4a4d63The furniture: frames, timestamps, key hints, and anything already dealt with.
bright black + dim#33364aAbsence — a day a thought did not come up on, and the oldest end of the trickle.
white · 7#b9bac6What Mooshik says, and ordinary body text.
white + dim#8f92a3Further down the list. Fading, not gone — it can climb back up.
bright white · 15#e9e9edNeom's own words, and whatever is strongest right now.
magenta · 5#9184d9Mooshik speaking, the focused panel, and the cursor. One accent, used as a line.
blue · 4#8d9ff0Something came back. Only ever on a thing returning from another day.
cyan · 6#7fc8d8Days and dates — the spine everything hangs from.
yellow · 3#cfa65cA caution worth hearing, and a day that was hard. Twice a week at most.
green · 2#6fae7fOne mark, one word: reachable, saved, keeping up. Never a sentence.
red · 1#d1656bReserved. Two uses in the whole app: a refused credential, and leaving a database behind.
bright magenta · 13#b3a7f2A key being pressed. Nothing else.
Held back on purpose: bright red 9, bright green 10, bright yellow 11, bright blue 12, bright cyan 14. An app that runs all day cannot spend every colour it owns.
How strength reads
▄▄▄▄▄▄▄Seven days, one mark each — filled on the days that thought came up. You can count them if you care; nobody makes you.
▄▄▁▄▄▄▁Five days. The dropped-to-baseline mark is a day it did not come up — absence, drawn rather than written.
▇   ▇   ·On the week screen the same marks sit directly under their day columns, so a thread lines up with the days it belongs to.
Aa Aa Aa AaBrightness is the gradient: strongest first, fading down. Four steps, no labels, no numbers, no tiers.
1↓Position does the rest. The list is always ordered by how often you return to something — there is no sort control, because there is nothing to sort by.
┌─┐A light frame is a panel. Accent frame means focused. Yellow frame is said once and needs no answer.
╔═╗A double frame appears exactly once, on the one action that cannot be undone by pressing esc.
Never on screen
No tier names, no scores, no percentages, and not one word from the engine underneath — no sessions, no nodes, no relevance, no promotion or decay. Strength is only ever justified with Neom's own history: “You've come back to this every day this week.”
