## 18:30 — Evening Bike Maintenance

Spent 45 minutes in the garage doing routine maintenance on the gravel bike after today's dusty 46km ride:

1. **Drivetrain Cleaning:** Applied citrus degreaser to the 11-speed chain, cassette, and derailleur jockey wheels. Scrubbed with a stiff brush and rinsed clean.
2. **Chain Lubrication:** Applied wet ceramic chain lube to each link roller, let it penetrate for 5 minutes, and wiped off the excess with a clean microfiber cloth.
3. **Gear Indexing:** The rear derailleur was shifting slightly slow on the 4th cog during the morning ride. Turned the barrel adjuster counter-clockwise a quarter turn to add cable tension. Shifts are crisp and instant across all 11 sprockets now.
4. **Tire Inspection:** Inspected the 38mm tubeless tires for thorn punctures or cuts. Sealant level checked good; zero air loss.
