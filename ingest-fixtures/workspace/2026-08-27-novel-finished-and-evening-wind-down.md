# Finished the novel, then Iris and Marcus came over

Closed the book this afternoon — actually finished it, which after three
weeks of reading four pages before falling asleep felt like an
accomplishment disproportionate to the actual size of the achievement.

## The book

I'd been picking away at it since early August, a slow-moving, fog-heavy
novel about a coastal town and a disappearance nobody in it seems to want
solved, more interested in atmosphere than plot. Read the last sixty pages
on the balcony before anyone arrived, which is the way I always mean to
read the ending of something and almost never actually manage. The ending
didn't resolve as much as I wanted, but I think that was the point, and
I'm the kind of reader who needs a day or two to decide whether that
annoys me or not. Right now, leaning toward not.

Already thinking about what's next. Might actually let the shelf sit empty
for a few days instead of immediately queuing something else, which is
not something I usually let myself do.

## Iris and Marcus

They came over around 7, the rescheduled version of the drinks I bailed on
mid-incident Wednesday — I'd sent Iris a one-line "sorry, on fire at work,
rain check" from the incident channel and felt bad about it all week.
Made the braise I keep saying I'll make and then don't, three hours, the
kind that makes the whole apartment smell like Sunday even on a Thursday.
Worth the time. We had the second half of a match on in the background
with the sound off, more company noise than anything either of us was
actually watching. Marcus gave me a hard time about being glued to my
phone Wednesday — fair, he wasn't wrong, I checked the incident channel
twice at dinner three weeks ago during a different, much smaller thing and
apparently it's a known pattern now. Iris asked about the visit-length
thing with my mom, I hadn't meant to bring it up but it came out anyway
somewhere around the second glass of wine, and it helped more than the
actual phone call this morning did, honestly. That's usually how it goes.

Good night. The kind where nobody looked at a phone from about 7:30 to 11,
which for this particular group is a genuinely rare outcome.

## Music

Put on an old ambient record I hadn't queued up in months while cooking,
before they arrived — one of those records I associate so strongly with a
different, calmer period that it almost felt like borrowing someone else's
evening. Switched to something louder once Marcus started arguing with
the TV.

## News, briefly

Had Mooshik pull a quick read on anything relevant while I was cooking —
nothing urgent, mostly the same cloud-capacity and AI-infrastructure-spend
chatter that's been the background noise of tech news for weeks now.
Nothing that touches Zephyr or Cobalt Lantern directly, which after this
week is exactly the kind of nothing I wanted to hear.

## Sleep, catching up

This is the first week in a while where I can feel the sleep debt actually
clearing instead of compounding. Sunday helped, tonight should help more
even with the later night than planned — Wednesday's adrenaline crash made
it obvious how much I've been running on fumes since the Windpipe rollout
started. Not waiting for the weekend to fix that this time.

## Net for the day

Quieter than Wednesday by every measure that matters, some of it genuinely
good, some of it just unresolved-but-not-actively-worse. I'll take it.
