## 18:30 — Friday evening ride home

Left the office at 18:15. The sun was beginning to dip behind the west hills, casting long amber light across the river.

The headwind from the morning had died down to a gentle 8 km/h breeze. Unlocked the gravel bike from the basement cage, clipped into pedals, and rode east along the waterfront trail. The river path had plenty of joggers, commuters, and families out enjoying the warm 21°C evening.

Rode at a relaxed, easy pace of 22 km/h, letting the week's cognitive load dissipate. Crossing back over the Morrison bridge, the water below looked calm and glassy.

Arrived home at 18:50. Parked the bike in the hallway, washed road dust off my arms, and poured a cold sparkling water with lime. Looking forward to cooking dinner, browsing flight routes to Bali, and disconnecting from work for the next two days.
