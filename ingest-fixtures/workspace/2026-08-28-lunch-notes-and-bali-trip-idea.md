## 12:45 — Lunch with Dax & Vacation Thoughts

Walked over to the food truck pod on 5th Avenue with Dax for lunch. Got chicken shawarma wraps and ate on the courtyard benches in the sun.

Dax was talking enthusiastically about his trip to Bali last year. He described staying in Ubud surrounded by lush jungle ravines, working from open-air bamboo cafes with high-speed fiber internet, and taking weekend trips down to the limestone cliffs of Uluwatu for surfing and sunset dinners on Jimbaran beach.

I haven't taken a real uninterrupted two-week holiday in over eighteen months. The intensity of debugging Windpipe and redesigning Cobalt Lantern over the past several weeks makes taking time off in late September feel very appealing.

Dax recommended visiting in September because it catches the tail end of the dry season with sunny days, low humidity, and pleasant 27°C sea breezes before the rainy season starts in late October.

Decided I'll spend some time this evening and over the weekend researching flight ticket prices to Denpasar (DPS), checking visa on arrival requirements, and putting together a rough 10-day itinerary.
