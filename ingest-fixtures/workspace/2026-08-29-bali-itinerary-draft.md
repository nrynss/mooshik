# Bali 10-Day Vacation Itinerary Draft (Ubud & Uluwatu)

**Date:** 2026-08-29 16:00  
**Author:** Neom  

Drafted a balanced 10-day itinerary split between the cultural/jungle highlands of Ubud and the coastal surf cliffs of Uluwatu.

## Itinerary Overview

### Part 1: Ubud (Days 1–5)
- **Base:** Guesthouse or eco-villa overlooking the Campuhan ridge or Penestanan rice terraces.
- **Activities:**
  - Morning runs along the Campuhan Ridge Walk.
  - Co-working and coffee at open-air bamboo cafes in Nyuh Kuning.
  - Day trip hike up Mount Batur crater for sunrise.
  - Exploring local art galleries and traditional gamelan performances.

### Part 2: Uluwatu & Bukit Peninsula (Days 6–10)
- **Base:** Cliffside bungalow near Bingin Beach or Padang Padang.
- **Activities:**
  - Surfing lessons and morning wave sessions at Padang Padang right.
  - Sunset dinners at cliffside warungs in Uluwatu overlooking the Indian Ocean.
  - Visiting the Uluwatu sea temple perched on the 70-meter limestone cliff.
  - Fresh grilled seafood dinner on the beach at Jimbaran Bay.

## Daily Budget Estimate
- Accommodation: $75/night (approx 1,150,000 IDR).
- Food & Coffee: $30/day (approx 460,000 IDR).
- Scooter rental & fuel: $8/day (approx 120,000 IDR).
- Total estimated on-ground spend for 10 days: ~$1,150 USD.
