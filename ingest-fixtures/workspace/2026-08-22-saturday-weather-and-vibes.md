twenty minutes of the ride and then didn't. Rode the long way to the
bridge and back, maybe 34km total, nothing serious, just legs and coffee
and not thinking about Windpipe for an hour. Wind was mild, out of the

stayed genuinely clear all day, not even the hazy kind of clear. Good day

## How the day actually went

Good day on balance, though not a clean one. Morning was the best part —
bike, then the Windpipe experiment, which is the kind of tinkering that
doesn't feel like work even though it technically is. Early afternoon dipped
a bit. My mother called around 2 while I was mid-experiment with the ring
occupancy numbers on screen, and I let it go to voicemail meaning to call
back in ten minutes once I'd finished the run I was on. Didn't call back
until almost 6. She didn't say anything about it when we finally talked, but
I could hear that she'd noticed, the way she always notices without saying
so directly, and now I've got that specific flavor of guilt sitting under
the rest of the day. She asked again about coming to visit next month and I
gave a non-answer about checking my calendar, which is true but also a
dodge, and we both know it.

I don't think this is really about the phone call. It's more that every
time we talk lately there's an undertone of "you've been busy for a while
now" that isn't wrong, and I don't have a good response to it that isn't
either defensive or a promise I'm not sure I'll keep. My brother's better
at just showing up for these calls without the guilt spiral, not sure what
he does differently. Might just be that he's not three time zones and a
six-day workweek away from things.

Anyway. Evening recovered — Farid texted asking if I was still in for
drinks and I said yes before I could talk myself into staying in. Details in
the other note, don't want to turn this one into a full log.

## Small good things, so this doesn't read as all complaint

- The bike route past the reservoir is still my favorite twenty minutes of
  any week.
- Windpipe experiment actually produced a clean, useful result on the first
  try, which almost never happens.
- Coffee was good today, which sounds trivial but a bad first cup can set a
  bad tone and today's was not that.
- Weather held exactly as predicted, no surprises, which after a week of
  everything at work being one surprise after another was its own kind of
  relief.

Net: a good day, with one part of it that's going to sit with me for a
while regardless. That seems normal enough not to worry about.
