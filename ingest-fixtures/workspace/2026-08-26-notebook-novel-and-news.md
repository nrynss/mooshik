## Ravi

Ravi texted twice today — once around noon, once around 6, both about
Ma's birthday next month and whether I'm coming. I saw both mid-incident
and mid-postmortem-draft respectively and answered neither. Not because
I don't know the answer, I do, I want to go, it's just that "yes, put me
down for it" requires actually looking at flights and days off and I did
not have the twenty minutes that takes today, and Ravi reads any delay as
me being noncommittal on purpose, which then reads to me as him assuming
the worst, and then we're both annoyed at each other over a text thread
that's really just two people who are both tired in their own ways.

This is the same argument we have every few months, just with the specific
event swapped out. He thinks I use work as an excuse. I think work is
genuinely the reason more often than he gives it credit for, today being
a fairly on-the-nose example, and also I think he's not entirely wrong
either, which is the annoying part. I didn't call Ma back today either —
different note, same evening, same underlying shape of the problem. Not
solving any of this tonight. Answering Ravi tomorrow with an actual date,
not a vague "probably," because the vague answers are the thing that
actually makes it worse and I know that and keep doing it anyway.
