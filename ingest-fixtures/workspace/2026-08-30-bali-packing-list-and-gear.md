# Bali Vacation Packing List & Gear Checklist

**Date:** 2026-08-30 09:45  
**Author:** Neom  

Drafted a minimalist packing checklist for the 10-day Bali trip in late September (aiming for carry-on only with a 35L backpack):

## Clothing & Apparel
- 4x breathable lightweight linen shirts.
- 3x merino wool t-shirts.
- 2x quick-dry boardshorts for surfing/swimming.
- 2x lightweight cotton shorts.
- 1x lightweight long-sleeve sun shirt (UPF 50+).
- 1x pair lightweight linen trousers (required for entering Balinese temples).
- 1x lightweight rain windbreaker.

## Electronics & Work Gear
- 13-inch laptop and compact 65W GaN USB-C charger.
- Universal travel plug adapter (Indonesia uses Type C and Type F European two-pin plugs).
- 10,000 mAh portable battery pack.
- Noise-canceling wireless earbuds.

## Travel Health & Documents
- Passport (valid through March 2027; exceeds 6-month requirement).
- Printed copy of e-VOA visa confirmation and travel insurance policy.
- Sunscreen (SPF 50 mineral reef-safe), mosquito repellent, oral rehydration salts.
- 20L waterproof dry bag for boat transfers and surf sessions.
