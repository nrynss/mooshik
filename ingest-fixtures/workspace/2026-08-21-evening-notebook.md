## The call I didn't take

My mother called around 7:30 while we were still at the table. Let it go
to voicemail, told myself I'd call back after, and then didn't, because by
the time we left it was after 9 and it felt too late to call and also I'd
had enough wine that I didn't trust myself to have that conversation
properly. She'll ask about the visit — she always does on Fridays now,
ever since the plan for me to come up got pushed twice already this
summer, once for the Cobalt Lantern launch and once because I was just
tired. I know the pushing looks like avoidance from her side even when it
isn't, and I know it's starting to actually be a little bit avoidance too,
which is the uncomfortable part. Calling her tomorrow, properly, not a
five-minute apology call, an actual call.

My brother texted separately asking if I'm "actually coming this time" for
the visit, which, fair, given the track record, but the phrasing still
got under my skin more than it should have. He's not wrong. Doesn't mean
it didn't sting a little reading it on the walk home.

Not going to pretend this is resolved. It isn't. Writing it down mostly so
I remember to actually make the call tomorrow instead of letting Saturday
slide into Sunday into "next week" the way it has the last three weekends.
