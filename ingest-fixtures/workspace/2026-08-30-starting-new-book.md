# Reading Notes: Starting 'A City on Mars'

**Date:** 2026-08-30 19:00  
**Author:** Neom  

Started reading *A City on Mars: Can we settle space, should we settle space, and have we really thought this through?* by Kelly and Zach Weinersmith.

## Initial Observations
The authors bring a delightfully skeptical, rigorously researched lens to the logistics of off-world settlements. 

The chapters on life support redundancy, closed-loop ecosystems, and international space law emphasize that the hardest parts of human settlement are not propulsion or rockets, but maintainability, failure modes, and institutional governance.

It is a refreshing contrast to tech-utopian hype. The parallels to distributed computing are obvious: building a fragile system that requires 100% uptime in a hostile environment is fundamentally an exercise in failure-domain isolation and defense-in-depth.

Enjoying the witty illustrations and crisp prose.
