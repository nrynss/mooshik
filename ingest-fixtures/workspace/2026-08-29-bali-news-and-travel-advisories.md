## Volcanic Activity Reports
- **Mount Agung:** Alert Level I (Normal / Green). No seismic swarms or eruptive activity reported in the past 60 days. Standard 2km exclusion radius around crater remains in place for hikers.
- **Mount Batur:** Alert Level I (Normal). Sunrise trekking tours operating normally with local guides.

## Weather & Ocean Conditions
- Current dry season weather patterns holding strong across south Bali and the central highlands.
- Swell forecasts for the Bukit Peninsula (Uluwatu, Padang Padang, Bingin) indicate clean 4-6 foot southwest swells with light offshore trade winds.
- Ubud daytime temperatures averaging 28°C with cooler 21°C evenings due to elevation.

## Entry Formalities
- Electronic Visa on Arrival (e-VOA) available through the official immigration portal (500,000 IDR).
- Bali regional tourist levy (150,000 IDR) payable online via the Love Bali system.
- Customs electronic declaration (e-CD) QR code generated 3 days prior to arrival.
