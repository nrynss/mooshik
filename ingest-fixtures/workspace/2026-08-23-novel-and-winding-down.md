## The novel

Still working through it — I'm somewhere past the two-thirds mark now, in the long stretch where the book stops introducing anything new and just starts paying off everything it planted two hundred pages ago. Read for about forty minutes on the balcony after the phone call home, which was exactly the right medicine for the mood that call left me in. A few chapters where two characters who've been circling each other the entire book finally have the conversation you've been waiting for, and it was written with more restraint than I expected — no big speech, just two people talking past each other slightly less than usual.

I keep almost dog-earing pages to remember a line and then deciding I'll just remember it, which I never do. Should actually start keeping a running note of favorite lines instead of trusting memory. Small thing to fix, not tonight.

Getting close enough to the end now that I can feel myself slowing down on purpose — the thing where you don't want a book to be over so you start reading fewer pages a night than you're capable of. Did that on purpose tonight, read less than I wanted to, stopped at a chapter break instead of pushing to the next one.
