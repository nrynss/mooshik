Long lunch with Dax and Ilana, 1pm at the usual place on the corner. Needed it more than I realized going in — hadn't seen either of them in about three weeks, which is too long given they live fifteen minutes away.

Was still a little wrecked from Saturday night, in the good way. We'd gone to see a friend-of-a-friend's band play a small venue downtown, stayed out later than any of us meant to, and I'm paying for it today in the form of moving slightly slower than usual and needing the lunch coffee more than the ride coffee this morning. Worth it — the band was better than expected, loud in the right way, and Ilana somehow knew half the room.

## Lunch itself

Sat outside for almost two and a half hours, which is the real marker of a good lunch — nobody checked the time, nobody had somewhere to be. Ordered too much food, as always happens when the three of us get together: a shared plate of something fried to start that none of us needed, then actual mains on top of that, then somehow dessert too because Ilana insisted the place does the coffee cake right and she wasn't wrong.

Dax is still deep in the process of trying to sell his old car, third buyer to fall through this month, and at this point it's become a running bit more than an actual problem — Ilana keeps offering increasingly ridiculous alternative solutions (currently: "just give it to a stranger and tell them it's a gift"). He's taking it well, mostly, though I suspect the fourth flaked buyer will be the one that finally breaks him.

Talked about the gig, talked about a football match next Saturday that we've now half-planned and will probably half-cancel the way we always do — Ilana's convinced we'll actually go this time, Dax and I have both said that before. There's a running joke by now about how many plans among the three of us actually survive contact with the following weekend. Last count, by Ilana's own tally, was something like one in four, which is generous.

Ilana's also in the middle of a work thing of her own — a manager who keeps changing the brief on a project two weeks before each deadline — and we spent a good ten minutes trading complaints back and forth like it was some kind of competitive sport. Neither of us won, if there was a prize it would've been a tie for "most tired of it."

I ended up venting more about work than I meant to. Been sitting with something for a few days — a design direction I put forward for how Windpipe should handle overload got relitigated in a meeting for the second time this month, same objections raised by the same person, like the first conversation never happened. I don't think anyone's acting in bad faith, but it's exhausting to keep re-making a case that was already made. Dax, who has zero context on any of this, just nodded along and said "sounds like nobody's writing anything down," which is probably more correct than he realizes.

Also mentioned — briefly, not wanting to make the whole lunch about it — that I'm feeling behind on things generally this week. Not in a crisis way, just the low hum of a backlog that didn't shrink no matter how many hours went into it. Ilana's response was some version of "that's every week though," which, fair. Dax offered to sell my problems along with his car, which got the biggest laugh of the afternoon.

## After

Walked around for a while after lunch instead of heading straight home, mostly to walk off the amount of food we ordered. Good day for it — the cloud had cleared completely by early afternoon and it was warm without being uncomfortable. Ended up outside a record shop for twenty minutes while Dax dug through a crate of nothing in particular and bought nothing, which is very on brand for him.

Made loose plans to do this again in two weeks instead of letting another three-week gap happen — we say this every time, so we'll see. Ilana's already threatening to make a recurring calendar invite for it, which honestly might be the only thing that actually works.

Glad I saw them. It's easy to let the work weeks eat the weekends too, and today didn't let that happen, mostly. Already half-committed to the football match next Saturday even though I give it maybe even odds of actually happening.
