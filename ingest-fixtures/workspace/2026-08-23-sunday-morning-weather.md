# Sunday, waking up slow

Woke up at 7:10, no alarm, which almost never happens. Overcast through the window, the kind of flat grey that could burn off or could sit there all day. Made coffee (too much, as usual — third cup by 9) and sat on the balcony for a while before deciding anything.

## Weather check

Pulled up the forecast on my phone instead of asking Mooshik, mostly out of habit:

- 7am: 18°C, overcast, wind out of the west at 11 km/h
- 11am: 21°C, breaking cloud
- 2pm: 26°C, mostly clear, light haze

Humidity sitting around 58% in the morning, dropping to the low 40s by afternoon. No rain in the forecast, which settled the bike-ride question before I'd even really asked it.

## Is it a good day

I think yes, cautiously. The overcast burned off exactly when the forecast said it would, which felt like a small win even though I had nothing to do with it. There's something about a grey morning turning into a clear afternoon that resets my mood more than a day that's clear from the start — like the day earned it.

Things going for today:
- No meetings, no Slack pings (checked once at 9, closed it, didn't open again)
- Legs feel good, no soreness from Thursday's ride
- Coffee didn't give me the jitters this time, don't know why
- The apartment is quiet — neighbors must be out

Things not going for it:
- Woke up thinking about the Windpipe backlog even though I told myself I wouldn't
- Ran out of the good coffee beans, down to the backup bag which is fine but not great
- Still haven't replied to Priya's message from Friday about the on-call rotation, low-grade guilt about it

Net: good day. Going to keep it that way by staying off my laptop until at least the evening, if I can manage it. Wrote this note by hand first and typed it up after, which felt like the right amount of effort for a Sunday.
