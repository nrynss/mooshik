# Wednesday evening, is it a good day

Short answer: no. First rough one in a while, and not because of any one
thing, more because the whole day stacked wrong from the start.

## Weather

- 7am: 16°C, low grey overcast, felt like rain was coming even before the
  forecast said so
- 10am: 17°C, light rain started around 10:20, right in the middle of the
  worst part of the incident, which I only know because I glanced out the
  window once during the mitigation window and it was just grey streaks
  on the glass
- 1pm: 18°C, steady rain, not heavy, the all-day kind
- 6pm: 17°C, still raining, no sign of it letting up before tomorrow
- No sunset to speak of, just the light draining out of the grey around
  7:40

Never even considered the bike today. Would have skipped it anyway for the
incident, but the rain made it a non-question instead of a discipline
question, which is almost a relief — one less thing to feel guilty about
today specifically, even though I'll feel guilty about the accumulated
missed rides again by the weekend, that's just how that works.

## The day, actually

Bad sleep going in — up twice overnight, running on maybe five hours
before three cups of coffee tried to cover for it. Then the pager at 9:42,
barely twenty minutes after standup, and from there it was heads-down
until past 11:30 without really noticing time pass, which is its own kind
of tired — the crash afterward is worse than if I'd been watching the
clock the whole way through.

Cancelled drinks with Marcus and Sana tonight. Had this on the calendar for
over a week — low-key thing, nothing special, just the three of us at the
place near Marcus's — and by 1pm I already knew I wasn't going to have anything
left for it. Had Mooshik help me get the text right, sent it a little after
2, Marcus wrote back "no worries, go rest" almost immediately, which was
kind and also somehow made me feel worse, not better. This is the second
time in maybe six weeks I've bailed on plans with them for work. I don't
think either of them is actually annoyed. I'm annoyed at myself on their
behalf, which isn't the same thing but sits in the same part of my chest.

Also missed a call from my mother around 10:30, right in the thick of
mitigation. Saw it on my watch, couldn't take it, told myself I'd call back
at lunch. Didn't. Told myself I'd call back after the incident closed.
Didn't do that either — by the time things were quiet enough I just didn't
have anything left to bring to that conversation, and calling her tired
and short felt worse than not calling at all. Going to try tomorrow.
Writing that down mostly so tomorrow-me has to look at it.

## Net

Not a good day. Not a disaster either — nobody's mad at me, the incident
resolved clean, the postmortem draft is in decent shape, Marcus and Sana
were gracious about it. It's more that the day cost more than it looks
like from the outside: work went fine in the end, and I still have nothing
left over for the people I actually wanted to see tonight. Going to eat
something that isn't standing over the sink, then bed early if I can
manage it, which given the last week is not a given.
