# Ride log — ridge trail loop

Left the apartment at 11:40, right as the cloud broke up like the forecast said it would. Took the long way out — coast road first, then cut up to the ridge trail instead of my usual weekday loop. Wanted distance today, not speed.

## Numbers

- Distance: 58.3 km
- Moving time: 2h 41m
- Average pace: ~21.7 km/h
- Elevation gain: 612 m
- Two stops: one at the overlook around km 22, one at the usual café at km 41

Legs felt strong for the first 35 km, then the climb out of the valley on the way back reminded me I hadn't done a ride this long in three weeks. Last 10 km were more about stubbornness than fitness.

## The ride itself

The coast road was nearly empty — a few other cyclists, one guy running with a dog who looked more tired than either of us. Wind was light out of the west most of the way out, which meant it was mostly at my back on the way home. I'll take it.

Stopped at the overlook for maybe ten minutes. Just stood there. There's a bench up there that somebody clearly maintains — always looks freshly painted — and I sat and ate a bar and didn't think about anything in particular, which was the whole point.

Café stop at km 41 for a flat white and a refill of the water bottle. Sat outside, watched the street for a bit. Didn't take my phone out once, which I'm oddly proud of given how this week has gone.

The climb up from the valley on the way back is the one part of this loop I actually respect. About 4 km at a grade that never lets you settle into a rhythm — just ramps and false flats. Standing on the pedals for most of the last kilometer. Worth it for the view at the top, even grey-tinged as it was.

## After

Home by 2:25, showered, ate an enormous plate of eggs and toast because I clearly underestimated how much that ride would take out of me. Legs are going to be sore tomorrow, which — fine, better than being sore from sitting at a desk all week.

Going to try to make this loop a monthly thing instead of something I do twice a year when the guilt piles up. The weekday loop is fine for maintenance but it doesn't scratch the same itch. There's a version of me that used to do this every other Sunday and I'd like to get back to being him.

Still a little wrecked from Saturday night too, if I'm being honest — Dax and Ilana and I were out late for a gig downtown, and doing 58 km the morning after was maybe not the smartest sequencing, but it felt good to push through it rather than use it as an excuse to stay in bed. Lunch with the two of them is later today, so there's a real chance I regret this decision by mid-afternoon.

Gear notes for future me: the rear derailleur was making a faint clicking sound on the climb that wasn't there last time, probably needs an adjustment before the next long ride. Bottle cage screw is also working itself loose again — third time this year, might just need a drop of thread locker instead of re-tightening it every few weeks and hoping.

One more thing worth writing down: somewhere around km 30 I found myself thinking through the Windpipe backpressure problem again, completely unprompted, while grinding up a hill. Didn't fight it, just let it run in the background and wrote a note to myself once I stopped at the overlook. More on that elsewhere — didn't want to let engineering thoughts take over this log.
