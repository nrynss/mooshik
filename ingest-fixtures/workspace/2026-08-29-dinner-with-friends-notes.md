## 20:00 — Dinner with Dax & Ilana

Met up with Dax and Ilana at *Khao San Kitchen* for dinner. We sat at an outdoor patio table under string lights. Ordered green curry with tofu, pad kee mao noodles, and crispy spring rolls.

Dax shared several practical tips for traveling in Bali based on his trip:
- **Scooter Safety:** Always wear a full-face helmet and keep an International Driving Permit handy. Traffic in Canggu can be chaotic, whereas riding around Uluwatu and the Bukit peninsula is more relaxed along the cliff roads.
- **SIM Cards / eSIM:** Recommended buying an Indonesian Telkomsel eSIM online before landing; good 5G coverage throughout Ubud and the Bukit.
- **ATMs:** Use official bank ATMs attached to Mandiri or BCA branches to avoid card skimming.
- **Cafes in Ubud:** Recommended *Seniman Coffee Studio* and *Clear Cafe* in Ubud for working and specialty coffee.

It was a great dinner and fun conversation. Feeling very excited about finalizing this trip.
