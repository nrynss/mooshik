## 11:45 — 45km Northern Trail Gravel Ride

Geared up with cycling jersey, bib shorts, helmet, and two full water bottles. Checked tire pressures on the gravel bike: 34 psi front, 37 psi rear for mixed pavement and packed gravel terrain.

Rode north out of the city along the river greenway. The first 15 kilometers were smooth asphalt path following the water. After passing the railway bridge, transitioned onto the hardpacked gravel river trail through the forest reserve.

The gravel was dry and dusty with occasional loose pea-gravel corners that tested cornering balance. Kept power steady at an endurance pace around 180W.

At the 25km mark, stopped at the river overlook bench to drink water and eat an energy bar. The view over the wide blue river canyon with evergreen pine forests on the opposite ridge was stunning in the midday sun.

Rode the remaining 20km loop back via the rural country roads. Total distance 46.2 km, elapsed time 1h 55m, average speed 24.1 km/h. Bike felt responsive and smooth throughout.
