## 12:30 — Sunday Batch Cooking & Meal Prep

Spent an hour and a half in the kitchen preparing healthy weekday lunches for the upcoming work week:

1. **Red Lentil Dal with Coconut Milk:**
   - Sautéed diced onions, minced garlic, fresh ginger, and green chilies in ghee.
   - Added ground cumin, coriander, turmeric, garam masala, and mustard seeds.
   - Simmered 2 cups of red lentils with chopped tomatoes, vegetable broth, and coconut milk until creamy.
   - Finished with fresh lime juice and chopped cilantro.
2. **Roasted Vegetable Medley:**
   - Tossed sweet potatoes, zucchini, cauliflower florets, and chickpeas with olive oil, smoked paprika, salt, and black pepper.
   - Roasted on two sheet pans at 200°C for 35 minutes until golden and caramelized.
3. **Grains:**
   - Cooked a pot of brown basmati rice in the rice cooker.

Portioned everything into four glass lunch containers for Monday through Thursday. Makes weekday lunch effortless during busy sprint days.
