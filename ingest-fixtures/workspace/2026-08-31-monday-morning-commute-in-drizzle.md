## 07:45 — Monday morning commute in drizzle

Left the apartment at 07:30 AM. As forecasted, a steady cool drizzle was falling with ambient air temperature at 16°C.

Wore the waterproof cycling jacket and helmet cover. The 38mm tubeless tires provided solid grip on the damp asphalt and wet metal expansion joints across the Morrison bridge. Road spray was kept off my work clothes by the full-coverage bike fenders.

Traffic was heavier than usual due to the wet weather, but the protected bike lane was clear. Arrived at the office at 07:55 AM. 

Stored the bike in the basement cage, changed into dry clothes, and walked up to my desk with a hot dark roast coffee from the 2nd floor espresso machine. 

Feeling alert and ready to kick off the Tidemark rollout with the team.
