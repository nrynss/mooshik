# Back on the bike

First real ride since Sunday, and I felt it.

## The day

Woke up around 6:50, better than I expected given how late I was up
rehashing yesterday's incident. Sky was doing that pale, thin thing it
does before it commits to being clear — by 8 it had committed. Checked the
forecast properly this time instead of just glancing out the window:

- 7am: 15°C, clear, light dew still on the railing
- 10am: 19°C, clear
- 1pm: 23°C, clear, barely any wind
- 5pm: 21°C, still clear
- Overnight low expected around 13°C

No humidity to speak of, which after Wednesday's sticky, on-edge feeling in
the incident channel felt almost aggressively pleasant. I don't think the
two things are related, but it felt like they were.

## The ride

Went out at 1:30 once the standup-equivalent chat with Priya wrapped
(she's fine, audit sink's been stable all morning, said it herself
unprompted which I appreciated). Same loop as always, out past the
reservoir and back, a little over 19km. Legs were stiffer than expected
for four days off — not sore exactly, just rusty, like the muscle memory
needed the first four or five kilometers to remember what it was doing.
Left my phone at home this time, actually did it, no checking at the one
stoplight on the descent like I always end up doing.

Average pace was slower than my usual, which I'm blaming on the week and
not on fitness, and I'm allowed to do that once.

## The family thing, since I need to write it somewhere

Mom called Wednesday at 09:44, right in the middle of the worst of it, and
I let it go to voicemail without even really registering whose name was on
the screen. Called back yesterday evening once things had settled and it
was short and a little strained — she wanted to talk about her and Dad
coming to visit next month, I was still half in incident-brain and gave
one-word answers, and she noticed, and said so, which is fair. Tried again
this morning properly, actual conversation, better but not fully repaired
— there's still an unresolved thing under it about how long the visit
should be, my sister apparently already said something to Mom about me
"always being too busy," which landed exactly the way it was probably
intended to. Didn't get into it today. Not avoiding it forever, just not
today.

## Is it a good day

Yes, and it's a specific kind of good — the relief kind, not the
everything-went-right kind. Nothing today was actually eventful. That's
the whole point. After Wednesday, "uneventful" is doing a lot of work.

Things going for it:
- Slept just over seven hours, first time this week I've cleared seven
- The ride happened, and it was easy to want to do it, which hasn't
  always been true after a rough day at work
- Both incident follow-up docs are basically done, so my head isn't
  holding them anymore
- Good coffee again — restocked yesterday evening without really
  planning to, just walked past the place on the way home
- Iris and Marcus rescheduled for tonight — the drinks I bailed on
  mid-incident Wednesday. Making up for it properly this time.

Things not going for it:
- Slight tightness in my right knee on the last climb, probably just the
  four days off, keeping an eye on it rather than worrying about it
- Still owe Priya a real reply about the on-call rotation, now a week and
  a half old, genuinely embarrassing at this point
- The call with Mom this morning helped but didn't fix anything, and I
  know I'm going to be thinking about the visit-length conversation on
  and off for the next few days whether I want to or not
- Mildly annoyed, still, about the platform review board wanting to
  relitigate a decision that already survived a live incident — wrote
  more about that in today's postmortem doc, don't need to repeat it here

Net: good day, quietly, with some things still unresolved underneath it.
Going to keep the evening simple before Iris and Marcus come over — no
laptop, no dashboards, though I said that Sunday too and broke it by 9pm
to check something that didn't need checking.

## Small thing worth writing down

Standing at the reservoir turnaround, it occurred to me that Wednesday's
incident is already starting to feel like something that happened to a
slightly different, more frazzled version of me. That gap between the
person mid-incident and the person on a bike three days later is probably
the whole reason recovery days matter, and I don't think I've ever
actually stopped to notice it happening before.
