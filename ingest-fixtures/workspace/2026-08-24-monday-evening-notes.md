# Monday, wrapping up

9:40pm. Sitting with the last of a glass of wine, decompressing before bed.
Rain actually showed up around 8, right on the 30% the forecast gave it,
which feels like a strange thing to be satisfied about but here we are.

## The day, honestly

It was a lot. Not a bad day exactly, but a full one in the way that leaves
me a little frayed by evening. Incident before I'd finished my first coffee,
a design review I had to steer back on track twice, a PR comment I had to
make basically the same point on for the third time this week, and a 1:1
where I had to admit out loud that the September roadmap doc doesn't exist
yet. None of it was a crisis. All of it together was tiring.

The Abhi 1:1 in particular sat with me longer than it should have. He was
fine about it — genuinely, no pressure from his side — but I built up more
dread about that conversation than it deserved, and I think that's on me,
not him. I keep doing this thing where I catastrophize a status update into
a performance review. Noting it because I want to stop doing that, not
because anything actually went wrong.

And I will say — mildly, because I already vented about it in the design
review notes — I'm tired of making the same argument twice in one week. We
settled the block-vs-drop question for Windpipe in writing on Friday. Having
to re-settle basically the same question for Cobalt Lantern today, out loud,
in a meeting, when the reasoning was already sitting in a doc, is the kind
of thing that doesn't ruin a day but does wear it down a little. Same with
Tomas asking the same question a third time. I don't think anyone's doing
anything wrong exactly. I just wish people read the thing before the
meeting about the thing.
